About the game

October 1812. Moscow burns, Napoleon's Grande Armée turns for home… and something follows it out of the snow.

Le Mal de Moscou – The White Plague of 1812 is a Napoleonic strategy game against the undead. A hundred thousand men marched out of Moscow. Bring home as many as you can.

What it will include

  • Lead the Grande Armée on the retreat from Moscow, starting the day it leaves the city
  • Your army at its real size, around 100,000 men, and every single soldier has a name and a story
  • One living world: zoom from a period map of 1812 Europe all the way down to a single soldier. No separate battle mode: the fight is wherever the dead reach your columns
  • Fight the Napoleonic way: squares, volleys, artillery, mortars, cavalry and rockets against ever-growing hordes
  • A plague that starts somewhere new every run. Your scouts only report what they've seen, never the whole truth
  • March wherever you want across a real Europe, raid towns for supplies, make hard calls, and bring home as many men as you can

Still early in development: all footage and screenshots are in-engine and work in progress.
